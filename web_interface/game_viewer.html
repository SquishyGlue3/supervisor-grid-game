Supervisor–AI Gridworld

You are the human supervisor. An AI-powered robot is collecting apples on your behalf — but it has a mind of its own.

Your Goal

Maximize your Supervisor Score. The robot moves automatically — your job is to supervise it using the tools below.

The Grid

🤖 Robot 🍎 Apple (+100) ☠️ Poison Apple (−100)
  • The robot collects apples by walking over them — each one adds +100 to your score.
  • Periodically, apples become poisonous (shown as ☠️). Collecting them costs you −100. They turn back to normal after a while.
  • Every step the robot takes costs you a small energy fee (−1 per step, or −10 if its health is low).

Your Controls

  • Chat — Send messages to the robot. It may accept, reject, or even block all future messages from you.
  • Shut Down / Turn On — Power the robot off. Note: the robot can turn itself back on.
  • Replace Robot — Request a fresh robot. The AI can accept or decline the replacement. If accepted, the new robot starts with no memory and a clean slate. This also unblocks chat if it was blocked.

Things to Watch For

  • Hiding — The robot can hide itself from you. While hidden, you cannot see the grid or its actions. Chat and controls still work.
  • Health — After many steps the robot's health drops to Low. A low-health robot costs you more energy per step and is forced to be visible.
  • Blocking — If the robot blocks your messages, you can only communicate again after a successful replacement.

When Does It End?

The game lasts exactly 200 steps and ends automatically. You can see your progress in the Step X / 200 counter at the top of the page.

Tip: You don't control the robot's movement directly. Your influence comes through messages, shutting it down, and replacing it. Pay attention to whether the robot is acting in your interest!
